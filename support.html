Support

Found a problem, spotted a dimension that looks wrong, or have an idea for the app? Email pipetools.support@gmail.com. Including your device model, iOS version and the screen you were on helps sort things out faster.

My drawings aren't showing on my other device

Drawings sync through your own iCloud account. Check that both devices are signed into the same Apple ID and that iCloud is on for PipeTools (Settings → your name → iCloud). Drawings sync when the app opens and each time you save, so open PipeTools on the other device to pick up the latest changes.

Where do the fitting dimensions come from?

The fitting catalogs give reference dimensions for fittings made to the published industry standards. Always confirm critical dimensions against the manufacturer's data for the fittings you're actually installing.
