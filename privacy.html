PipeTools Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

The short version

PipeTools doesn't collect your personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the developer can't see your drawings, fittings or photos.

What's saved on your device

Your drawings (including any background photos), custom fittings, the photos and PDFs you add to the Photos and Documents folders, sketches and settings are saved on your device so they're there next time you open the app.

iCloud sync

If you're signed into iCloud, your drawings, their background photos, your custom fittings and the contents of your Photos and Documents folders are also saved in your own private iCloud account, so they appear on your other devices signed into the same Apple ID. Apple stores this data under Apple's privacy policy, and it counts toward your iCloud storage. The developer has no access to it. You can turn iCloud off for PipeTools in your device's iCloud settings; the app then keeps saving on the device only.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to: to measure slope with augmented reality, and to take a background photo for a drawing. The slope camera view is processed live on your device and isn't recorded or sent anywhere. A background photo you take is saved with the drawing as described above.

Photos

When you add a background photo or add photos to the Photos folder, the system photo picker shares only the photos you pick. PipeTools can't see the rest of your library. PDFs you add come only from files you pick.

Exports and sharing

PDFs and other exports go only where you send them, through the system share sheet.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. The developer doesn't receive your payment details.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's privacy settings, Apple may send the developer anonymous crash and performance reports. These don't identify you and are used only to fix problems.

Deleting your data

Deleting a drawing, custom fitting, photo or document in the app removes it from this device and from iCloud, and so from your other devices. Deleting the app removes everything saved on the device. To remove PipeTools' data from iCloud as well, use the storage settings for your Apple ID's iCloud account.

Changes

This policy may be updated with the app. The date above shows when it last changed.

Contact
